# Azamat Shmitov

MSc student in Data Science and Engineering at Politecnico di Torino (Turin, since September 2026). Before that I did a B.Eng. in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Nazarbayev University (2026).

Most of my work is computer vision for football: finding a ball that is 10 to 40 pixels wide and often blurred, and detecting players on a fixed panoramic camera. Since July 2025 I have also worked as an AI engineer for the Freedom QJ League, Kazakhstan's national youth football league.

### Publications

### Public projects
- [pixellot](https://github.com/outprouded/pixellot): player and ball detection on a fixed 3840×800 panoramic camera that covers the whole pitch, fine-tuned on our own match footage. Work in progress.
- [InDrive-Hackathon](https://github.com/outprouded/InDrive-Hackathon): vehicle damage and cleanliness classification from car photos (YOLOv8, ViT, VGG). Top 5 of 4,800 at Decentrathon 4.0.
- [qj-assistant](https://github.com/outprouded/qj-assistant): a Streamlit app that answers questions about league statistics in plain language.
